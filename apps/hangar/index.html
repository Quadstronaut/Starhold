Shush

Open · free Unknown

Open

A daily word game on Discord: one word is forbidden each day, and saying it in the server puts you on the list.

shushgame.com

Free. The public list shows a display name, the word and the date, for thirty days, then it drops off. Use /hide-me in Discord to leave every list we keep; we also edit the bot's own recent messages. Copies kept by Discord or other players, and older bot messages, can't be changed.
