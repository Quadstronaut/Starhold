How I run them

Your data, your call

The same rules for every service and every person, wherever you live.

  • Privacy

    No trackers. Logs only as far as running the service needs. Your data is never sold. Notifications are opt-in; only critical account and service notices are not.

  • Export and delete

    One click to export everything held on you, one click to delete it and the account with it. That is the standard each account is being built to; until it ships, ask through the contact form.

  • Fair terms

    No dark patterns, no expiring credits, no surprise charges. Plain-English terms and a public price table wherever something costs money.
