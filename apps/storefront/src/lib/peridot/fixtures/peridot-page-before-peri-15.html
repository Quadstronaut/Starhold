=====

Peridot

What it is

Peridot is a period tracker for Android. It keeps everything on your phone, encrypted. What you share with a circle travels encrypted through relays, one of them run by Starhold, and Starhold cannot read it.

Peridot is free. There is no price, no purchases and no ads.

Why it exists

After the Dobbs decision in 2022, period tracking data became a legal risk. Most apps store it on company servers, where it can be subpoenaed, sold or breached, and their answer was a privacy policy that can be changed at any time.

Peridot takes a different approach. It keeps no readable cycle data anywhere a company or government can reach.
