2026-11-14

**What changed.** Sign-in and paid checkout are open on four services: [Render](/services/render/), [Status](/services/status/), [Umbermark](/services/umbermark/) and [Zenithmark](/services/zenithmark/). You sign in with a [Starhold Account](https://starship.starhold.app), which uses Google sign-in and has no separate password. Paid plans are sold through Polar, the merchant of record. The terms, the privacy policy and the refund terms on starhold.dev carry the launch wording.

**Prices.** Render: Free, Hobby $5, Pro $19 and Scale $49 a month. Status: Free, Private $15 and MSP $29 a month. Umbermark: free scans, a $5 one-off report, and Solo $9, Pro $19 and Agency $49 a month. Zenithmark: a free scorecard, Monitor $9 a month for 1 domain and Monitor 5 $29 a month for 5 domains.

**What it means for you.** Free tiers cost nothing. Cancel a paid plan any time: run out the period or take a prorated refund. If a $5 Umbermark report fails or is not what we described, ask within 14 days and we refund it in full. From your Starhold Account you can export what Starhold holds on you or delete your account. Status pages send email updates to subscribers who confirm.

**What is still not done.** Notification controls you can switch off, offline tiers that run on your own machine, and one public price table are not built. Starbot and QNix are in development. Umbermark email alerts on a score drop do not send yet.
