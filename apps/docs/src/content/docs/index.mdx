<section class="section section-divided" aria-labelledby="rules-title">
	<h2 id="rules-title">The <span class="accent-word">rules</span></h2>
	<p class="lede">These are the rules every Starhold service is being built to. Not every service meets all of them yet: each page in Services lists what is not there yet.</p>
	<div class="grid">
		<article class="fleet-card"><h3>Your data stays yours</h3><p>No logs beyond what it takes to run the service. Nothing kept that is not needed. Your data is never sold.</p></article>
		<article class="fleet-card"><h3>Export and delete</h3><p>Built to give you one click to download everything held on you and one click to delete it, account and all. Where that is not built yet, ask and it is done by hand.</p></article>
		<article class="fleet-card"><h3>No dark patterns</h3><p>No expiring credits, no surprise charges, no auto-upgrade. Where paid plans open, cancelling is built to be one click with a prorated refund.</p></article>
		<article class="fleet-card"><h3>One public price table</h3><p>A generous free tier, every plan and limit on one page, and the cost basis published once it is measured.</p></article>
		<article class="fleet-card"><h3>No trackers</h3><p>No analytics, pixels, tag managers or third-party scripts. Terms in plain English.</p></article>
		<article class="fleet-card"><h3>Notifications you chose</h3><p>Opt-in by default. Only critical account and service notices are opt-out. Nothing is marketing.</p></article>
	</div>
	<p class="lede">Offline tiers that run on your own machine, with nothing for Starhold to hold at all, are the premium privacy option. They are coming soon.</p>
</section>
