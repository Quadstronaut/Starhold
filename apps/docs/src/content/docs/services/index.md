Starhold hosts and operates these services for people who care where their data goes. Most of it is **open**; the rest is **in development**. Open means you can use it now. Sign in with a Starhold Account; free tiers are available and paid plans are on sale. Each page says what the service does today, how to get in, and which of the [Starhold rules](/) it already meets.

| Service | What it is | State today | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Umbermark](/services/umbermark/) | Scored server hardening report with a shareable link | Open · paid plans | Open: free scans; report $5 one-off; Solo $5, Pro $19, Agency $49 a month |
| [Zenithmark](/services/zenithmark/) | Mail and DNS scorecard for a domain | Open · paid plans | Open: free scorecard; monitoring $5 or $19 a month |
| [Solatype](/services/render/) | OG image, PDF and screenshot API | Open · paid plans | Open: free tier; Hobby $5, Pro $19, Scale $49 a month |
| [Heliostat](/services/status/) | Private and white-label status pages for MSPs | Open · paid plans | Open: free tier; Private $5, MSP $19 a month |
| [Echo](/services/echo/) | What the internet sees when you connect: your address, headers and TLS details | Open · free | Open: free, no account |
| [Starbot](/services/starbot/) | One bot, run by Starhold. Add it to your Discord server in one click. | Open · paid plans | Open: free to add; Plus $5, Pro $19 a month, two servers each |
| [Shush](/services/shushgame/) | The quiet game on Discord | Open · free | Open |
| [DysonSphere](/services/dysonsphere/) | Personal hosting, built to be hard to break | In development | Interest form only |

## Starhold Suite

The Starhold Suite is one plan at $49 a month. It includes the middle plan of every tool: Umbermark Pro, Zenithmark Monitor 5, Heliostat MSP, Solatype Pro, and Starbot Pro (two servers). You buy it from the [Starhold Account page](https://starship.starhold.app/account/subscriptions). Single plans stay available.

## Getting in

Sign in with your [Starhold Account](https://starship.starhold.app) with Google. Free tiers are open. Paid plans are on sale. These rules apply to every purchase. Questions: use the contact form at [starhold.dev/contact](https://starhold.dev/contact) or write to [hello@starhold.dev](mailto:hello@starhold.dev).

Whether each service is up right now: [Fleet status](https://status.starhold.fyi).
