**A scored hardening report for your server, with a link you can share.** Run a scan, get a score and a report that explains each finding.

- **State:** live, early access.
- **Where:** [umbermark.starhold.app](https://umbermark.starhold.app)

## How to get in

A free scan needs no account. A full report is a one-off purchase, and plans are sold through Polar; the [pricing page](https://umbermark.starhold.app/pricing) lists what each includes. Accounts are by invitation: ask through the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact?intent=invite) and I send your sign-in link.

## Fair terms and privacy defaults

- **Credentials:** never stored for one-off scans. A saved server keeps an encrypted key.
- **Report links:** anonymous report links expire after 30 days. Reports are not indexed by search engines.
- **Cancel:** through the Polar customer portal.
- **Your data:** to see or delete what Umbermark holds on you, use the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact) or write to [hello@starhold.dev](mailto:hello@starhold.dev). One-click export and delete are not built here yet.

## Not there yet

- **One-click export and delete** are not built. Ask, and it is done by hand.
- **Email alerts** on a score drop do not send yet.
