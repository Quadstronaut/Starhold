**A scored hardening report for your server, with a link you can share.** Run a scan, get a score and a report that explains each finding.

- **State:** Open · paid plans.
- **Availability:** Open: free scans; report $5 one-off; Solo $9, Pro $19, Agency $49 a month.
- **Where:** [umbermark.starhold.app](https://umbermark.starhold.app)

## How to get in

A free scan needs no account. A full report is a $5 one-off purchase. Monthly plans are Solo at $9 (up to 7 saved servers, re-scans weekly at most), Pro at $19 (up to 15, daily at most) and Agency at $49 (up to 50, daily at most); for more than 50 servers, Enterprise is sized by email to hello@starhold.dev. All are sold through Polar, and the [pricing page](https://umbermark.starhold.app/pricing) lists what each includes. Sign in with your [Starhold Account](https://starship.starhold.app) to buy and manage plans. Paid plans are on sale. These rules apply to every purchase.

## Fair terms and privacy defaults

- **Credentials:** never stored for one-off scans. A saved server keeps an encrypted key.
- **Report links:** anonymous report links expire after 30 days. Reports are not indexed by search engines.
- **Cancel:** through the Polar customer portal.
- **Refunds:** cancel a monthly plan any time for a prorated refund. If a $5 report fails or is not what we described, ask within 14 days and we refund it in full. The [refund terms](https://starhold.dev/legal/refunds) apply.
- **Your data:** to see or delete what Umbermark holds on you, write to [privacy@starhold.dev](mailto:privacy@starhold.dev) or use the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact). Export and delete are in your [Starhold Account](https://starship.starhold.app).
