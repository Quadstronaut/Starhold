**A scored hardening report for your server, with a link you can share.** Run a scan, get a score and a report that explains each finding.

- **State:** Open · early access, paid plans.
- **Availability:** Free scans open; purchases paused.
- **Where:** [umbermark.starhold.app](https://umbermark.starhold.app)

## How to get in

A free scan needs no account. Checkout is paused; nothing is for sale today. These rules apply when it reopens. A full report is a one-off purchase, and plans are sold through Polar; the [pricing page](https://umbermark.starhold.app/pricing) lists what each includes. No sign-in is open today. Accounts are by invitation: ask through the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact?intent=invite) and we reply by hand.

## Fair terms and privacy defaults

- **Credentials:** never stored for one-off scans. A saved server keeps an encrypted key.
- **Report links:** anonymous report links expire after 30 days. Reports are not indexed by search engines.
- **Cancel:** through the Polar customer portal once checkout reopens.
- **Your data:** to see or delete what Umbermark holds on you, write to [privacy@starhold.dev](mailto:privacy@starhold.dev) or use the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact). Export and delete are by request to privacy@starhold.dev, done by hand within 30 days; one-click export and delete are not built here yet.

## Not there yet

- **One-click export and delete** are not built. Ask, and it is done by hand.
- **Email alerts** on a score drop do not send yet.
