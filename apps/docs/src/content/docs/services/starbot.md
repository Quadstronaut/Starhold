**Your own Discord bot, with your name and avatar, set up and run for you.**

- **State:** Open · paid plans.
- **Availability:** Open: Custom Bot $5, Custom Bot + Ops Pack $19 a month.
- **Where:** [dash.starhold.app](https://dash.starhold.app)

## How to get in

Go to [dash.starhold.app](https://dash.starhold.app) and sign in with your Starhold Account. Pick the features you want, subscribe, then give Starbot your own Discord bot's token. Starbot runs the bot for you under your bot's name and avatar. Each account can have one bot.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Custom Bot | $5 a month | One bot with the standard features. |
| Custom Bot + Ops Pack | $19 a month | One bot, including the operations features. |

The [Starhold Suite](/services/) ($49 a month) includes one Starbot Custom Bot with the Ops Pack, with no separate checkout. Plans renew monthly until cancelled. Payment is taken by Polar, our merchant of record. Manage or cancel a plan bought on the dashboard from its Manage billing link, which opens Polar's customer portal. Cancel a Suite on your Starhold Account page. Refunds are paid less the payment processing cost. Full details in the [Refund Policy](https://starhold.dev/legal/refunds).

## Fair terms and privacy defaults

Starbot runs under the same [Starhold rules](/) as every other service. Questions about what it holds: use the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact) or write to [hello@starhold.dev](mailto:hello@starhold.dev).

## What Starbot keeps

As built today, Starbot keeps your Starbot account (Discord id, name and avatar, and the plan, features, bot name and bot avatar you chose), the server settings and Discord ids its features need to work, your bot's Discord token (stored encrypted), keys you add for other services (stored encrypted), and contact notices. If you turn on the logging feature, the text of each new message is held in memory for up to 24 hours so a deleted or edited message can be shown in your log channel. Starbot also fetches the web addresses you ask it to watch.

**When your bot is removed, its data is deleted.** A bot is removed when its plan ends (you cancel, a payment stays unpaid for 7 days, or your Starhold Suite ends), when no bot token is given within 72 hours of paying, or when you ask. The bot's token, keys, settings and stored data are then deleted from our server and from its backup copy, normally within one hour. A backup of our list of bots keeps the bot's name and avatar link for up to 15 days after that. Cached message text is dropped as soon as the bot stops. What the logging feature posted in your log channel is in your own Discord server: removing the bot does not delete it.

**What is kept.** Your Starbot account and purchase records stay until you ask us to delete them (backups drop them within 15 days after that). Deleting your Starhold Account removes only its link to Starbot, and ends a bot your Starhold Suite paid for. Polar keeps its own payment records as merchant of record. Server logs hold ids, events and errors, never message text or tokens, for 30 days. Contact notices have no automatic deletion yet: a 12-month purge is not built. Ask and they are deleted by hand. The [privacy policy](https://starhold.dev/legal/privacy) says the same.
