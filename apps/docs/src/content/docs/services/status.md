**Status pages for MSPs: public, password-protected or white-label.**

- **State:** Invitation only.
- **Where:** [status.starhold.app](https://status.starhold.app)

## How to get in

Sign-in and checkout are not open yet. Invitations are sent by hand: ask through the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact?intent=invite) and we send your link. Paid plans are not open.

## What works today

A free public page with components, incidents, scheduled maintenance and monitors, plus RSS, Atom, a JSON endpoint and a badge. Monitors can take webhook and heartbeat check-ins. Pages can be password-protected. Email updates to subscribers are not live yet.

## Fair terms and privacy defaults

- **No trackers,** including on your customers' pages.
- **Your content is never logged:** incident text, passwords, tokens and check bodies stay out of the logs.
- **Export and delete:** "Download my data" builds the file while you wait; "Delete account" and "Delete page" act at once.
- **Hard caps, clear messages:** every limit names itself and its value.

## Not there yet

- **Paid plans and billing** are not open, so cancel and refund terms do not apply yet.
