**A mail and DNS scorecard for any domain.** Enter a domain and get a report on SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX, MTA-STS, TLS, DNSSEC, CAA and BIMI.

- **State:** Open · paid plans.
- **Availability:** Open: free scorecard; monitoring $9 or $29 a month.
- **Where:** [zenithmark.starhold.app](https://zenithmark.starhold.app)

## How to get in

The scorecard needs no account. Open the site and enter a domain. Daily monitoring with the DMARC report inbox is a paid plan: Monitor is $9 a month for 1 domain and Monitor 5 is $29 a month for 5 domains, on the [plans page](https://zenithmark.starhold.app/plans). Sign in with your [Starhold Account](https://starship.starhold.app) to buy and manage a plan. Paid plans are on sale. These rules apply to every purchase.

## Fair terms and privacy defaults

- **No trackers.** The scorecard needs no account and sets no cookies. Signing in for monitoring sets a session cookie and an anti-forgery cookie; both go when you sign out and expire after 30 days.
- **Reports** are kept for 30 days, then removed, and are not indexed by search engines.
- **Mail:** the scorecard sends none. Monitoring emails you alerts and a weekly summary about the domains you added, delivered by Postmark.
- **Your data:** monitoring data is exported or deleted in your [Starhold Account](https://starship.starhold.app); removing a domain erases its history at once. For anything else Zenithmark holds, use the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact) or write to [hello@starhold.dev](mailto:hello@starhold.dev).
