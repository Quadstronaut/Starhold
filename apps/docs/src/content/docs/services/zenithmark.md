**A mail and DNS scorecard for any domain.** Enter a domain and get a report on SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX, MTA-STS, TLS, DNSSEC, CAA and BIMI.

- **State:** Open · free.
- **Where:** [zenithmark.starhold.app](https://zenithmark.starhold.app)

## How to get in

No account and no invitation needed. Open the site and enter a domain.

## Fair terms and privacy defaults

- **No accounts, no cookies, no trackers.**
- **Reports** are kept for 30 days, then removed, and are not indexed by search engines.
- **No mail:** Zenithmark never sends email.
- **Your data:** to see, correct or delete what Zenithmark holds, use the [contact form](https://starhold.dev/contact) or write to [hello@starhold.dev](mailto:hello@starhold.dev).
